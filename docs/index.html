Conceptual Investigations Unit

The Conceptual Investigations Unit is a research consortium, coordinated by Ken Caldeira, developing information and conceptual frameworks that can broadly contribute to improving human welfare while protecting natural ecosystems.

We perform quantitative calculations to reach qualitative conclusions.

What We Do

Conceptual analysis, quantitative modeling, and interdisciplinary collaboration on climate, energy, and society.

Who We Are

The people of the consortium, drawn together by problems that are global in scale and span many decades.

What We Publish

Peer-reviewed publications, each with the question it addressed, what it found, and why that matters.

What is CIunit.org?

What this site is for, and how the Unit relates to the Sustainable Solutions Lab at Stanford University.

The Sustainable Solutions Lab at Stanford University is almost always able to hire as postdoctoral research scientists excellent early career researchers whose perspectives and preferred research approaches are aligned with those of the Conceptual Investigations Unit.

The Conceptual Investigations Unit is hosted by the Sustainable Solutions Lab at Stanford University.
