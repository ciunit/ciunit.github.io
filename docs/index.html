Conceptual Investigations Unit

The Conceptual Investigations Unit is a research consortium developing information and conceptual frameworks that can broadly contribute to improving human welfare while protecting natural ecosystems.

We perform quantitative calculations to reach qualitative conclusions.

What We Do Who We Are

The Sustainable Solutions Lab at Stanford University is almost always able to hire as postdoctoral research scientists excellent early career researchers who are aligned with the perspectives and approaches of the Conceptual Investigations Unit. For further information, contact Steve Davis and/or Ken Caldeira. (18 months before you get your PhD is not too early to contact us.)

The Conceptual Investigations Unit is hosted by the Sustainable Solutions Lab at Stanford University.
